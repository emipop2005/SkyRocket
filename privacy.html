Sky Rocket — Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 November 2026

Sky Rocket is an independently developed Android game (package com.retro.skyroute). The developer can be contacted at SkyRocket.game.support@gmail.com.

Game data and controls

Sky Rocket stores settings, progress, credits, unlocked ships, purchase entitlement information and personal records locally on your device. Motion sensor readings are processed locally to steer the spaceship when tilt controls are enabled.

Advertising and consent

Sky Rocket uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. Google advertising services may collect or receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, advertising interactions, crash logs and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Information may be shared with Google and applicable advertising partners. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertising requests. You can revisit available consent choices through Privacy Options in the game settings. Purchasing PRO removes in-game advertisements. See how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Google Play Billing processes purchases of credits, ships, PRO, fuel and Marathon continues. Google handles payment details; the game receives product identifiers, purchase tokens and transaction status to deliver purchases and restore eligible permanent entitlements. The developer does not receive your full payment card details. Purchase records are retained by Google according to its policies, and local fulfillment records remain on your device until app data is cleared.

Online rankings and cloud saves

Online leaderboards and cloud saves are not currently enabled. This policy will be updated before those services are introduced.

Legal bases and security

Where applicable, processing is based on providing requested game features and purchases, consent for advertising processing where required, legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations. Communications with Google services use encrypted transport. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until cleared through Android app settings or removed by uninstalling, subject to any device backup or restore settings. If you email support, your message and email address are used to respond to your request and retained as needed to resolve it and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by the developer using the contact below.

Privacy rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact SkyRocket.game.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Changes

This policy will be revised when the game's data practices change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
