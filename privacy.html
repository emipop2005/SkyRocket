Sky Rocket — Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 November 2026

Sky Rocket is an independently developed Android game (package com.retro.skyroute). The developer can be contacted at SkyRocket.game.support@gmail.com.

Current testing version

The game stores settings, progress, credits, unlocked ships and personal records locally on your device. Motion sensor readings are used to steer the spaceship when tilt controls are enabled. These readings are processed on the device for gameplay. The current testing version does not implement live purchases, advertising or online leaderboards.

Planned online services

Future versions are intended to integrate Google Play Billing for purchases, Google Play Games Services for optional sign-in and public rankings, and Google AdMob for advertising. These integrations may process purchase identifiers, gaming profile information, scores, device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics and advertising interaction information, depending on the final configuration. This policy will be updated before those services are enabled to explain actual collection, sharing, purposes, retention and applicable consent choices. See Google's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until cleared through Android app settings or removed by uninstalling, subject to any device backup or restore settings. If you email support, your message and email address are used to respond to your request and retained as needed to resolve it and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by the developer using the contact below.

Privacy rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact SkyRocket.game.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Changes

This policy will be revised when the game's data practices change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
